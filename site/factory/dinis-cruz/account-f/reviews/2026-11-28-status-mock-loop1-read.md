## Unclear
1. **Do the cards say what "yes" costs?** Card 2 ("Start the London strikes session") never says what London strikes is, and "If you wait: The strikes page waits" only repeats the title. Starting a session is a spend and a commitment; add one line of what saying yes does and costs.
2. **The answer box mixes the words to say with the explanation.** Card 1's box holds the quote, then "Attaches the repository only; widens nothing (content/about/** stays held under P11 h; no content/index.md). Fallback: ... add_repo". I cannot tell whether Copy copies all of it or only the quote. Say exactly: the quote only in the box and on Copy; the explanation and the fallback in Details.
3. **Card 3 has two answers behind one Copy.** Two quoted options, one button: which does it copy? Give each option its own button, or make it two plain choices. The title is also a two-part question, 17 words long.
4. **Session ids and insider words are still on the card face**: "session_016CrbJLv54Kh4CSTzagW2G3" on cards 1 and 2, "P11 h", "add_repo". The note's own rule puts these in Details. Show "in the cl-6.maestro chat" with the id behind a link.
5. **"(example)" after the next-briefing time, and the grey line at the top ("design mock, loop 1 ... to confirm with cl-6.decisions")** are build notes; they must not ship, and "example" will make a real time look fake.
6. **The counts look like buttons** (the "5 now" box has a heavy border like a selected tab) but nothing says they are or are not tappable. Either make them links to their lists or style them as plain figures.
7. **5 + 14 + 9 is 28; the note says the file holds 29 questions.** Say what is left out (one answered? information, not a decision?), or a reader who counts will stop trusting the counts.

## Missing

He will answer, reload and see the same card, and may answer twice. Say when the page next updates, and show an "answered, waiting for the next update" state if the answer can be seen anywhere. This is the first first-use trap.
- **The age of each ask on the card face** ("waiting 3 days"); it is only in Details now. An old ask feels different from a new one.
- **What each decision unblocks** ("holds up 3 agents"); the page's own baseline has 17 agents blocked, and a decision's size is the reader's best way to rank it.
- On desktop: the right rail with the counts, the next briefing and the "Running fine" counts is in the note but not in the 1280 shot, which is a single 760 px column with empty sides. Not judged: whether it is built.
